# First-pass selection policy

This is a **provisional author-name filter**, not a definitive historical classification.

## Review queue

- 庾信: Liang-origin poet with substantial Northern-period compositions; classify at poem level.
- 蕭詧: Western Liang ruler and contested Southern/Northern political classification; verify scope and poem dates.
- 陸凱: name ambiguity / Northern Wei association; verify identity.
- 蘇小小: attribution and dating uncertain.
- 無名氏: cannot assign to Southern courts on author name alone.

## Excluded at first pass

薛道衡 (Sui), 温子昇, 邢邵, 魏收, 陽休之, 李爽, 王褒 (Northern-period career). **王褒 may require poem-level reinspection for pre-Northern work.** All excluded records are preserved.

## Next checks

Validate every author's dates, distinguish place of composition from birthplace, resolve duplicate works, identify historical sources, and keep ambiguous works in the review queue rather than silently discarding them. Do not infer a tonal category from modern Mandarin pronunciation.
